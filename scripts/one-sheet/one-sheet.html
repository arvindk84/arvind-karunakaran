Signature framework

The Workflow Redesign Playbook

Redesigning work around AI with the people who do it

Teams map a workflow task by task, decide where AI should substitute for, augment, or expand the work, and then redesign roles, hand-offs, decision rights, and metrics with the people who do it, so the change holds after the pilot ends. It is the core of his in-house workshops with leadership teams.
